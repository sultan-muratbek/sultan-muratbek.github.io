Hi there! 👋 Thanks for stopping by.

I am a senior at UC Berkeley, majoring in Computer Science (Honors Program) and Legal Studies. You can find my coursework [here](https://sultan-muratbek.github.io/markdown/).

I love teaching and mentoring—I've served on the course staff for CS 61A for five semesters now and for CS 61C for one semester as part of the CS Mentors community. My research interests are in database systems. I currently do research with Professor Alvin Cheung, including [Concord](https://concord-db.github.io/), a video relational algebra for cross-modal query optimization.

Outside of my studies, I enjoy playing pickleball and soccer, and I love going on long walks.

If our interests overlap or you'd like to connect, feel free to reach out!
